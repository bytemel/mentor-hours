Mentor Hours

Office hours that book themselves

Mentors open slots. Founders book with one link. Two people can never book the same slot, even if they press Book at the same second.

Try it

The demo hub has three mentors and fresh slots for the next five weekdays. Book one, then try booking it again from another tab.

Make your own board

No sign-up. You get a private manage link: keep it, it is the only key.

How it works

  1. Create a board and keep the manage link.
  2. Open slots for each mentor: date, time, length, topic.
  3. Share the public link in the cohort WhatsApp group.
  4. Founders book with a name and a phone or email. Each gets a calendar file and a cancel link.
  5. The manage view shows who booked what and what they want help with.

Times are East Africa Time. Data sits in Neon Postgres. Boards with no use for 90 days are deleted.
